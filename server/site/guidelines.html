Community guidelines

Last updated November 21, 2026

It’s a Lifestyle is for real neighbors. Treat people the way you would on your own street.

Not allowed

  • Harassment, bullying, threats, or encouraging violence.
  • Hate speech or attacks based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or national origin.
  • Sexual content involving anyone under 18. We report this to the authorities.
  • Accounts for anyone under 18. This app is for adults.
  • Sexual or violent images sent to people who didn’t ask for them.
  • Sharing someone’s address, phone number or other private details without their permission.
  • Pretending to be someone else.
  • Selling illegal goods or services, scams, and spam.
  • Anything else that breaks the law.

How rules are enforced

Messages are end-to-end encrypted, so we can’t read them. Instead:

  • Block: open a chat’s details and choose Block. You won’t receive anything more from that person.
  • Report: long-press a message, or open a plan and choose “Report this plan.” The report, including the reported content, goes encrypted to the neighborhood’s owners.
  • Owners act: owners can remove someone from the neighborhood. That account can’t rejoin, and owners can make a new join code so the old one stops working for anyone.
  • Tell us: for serious problems, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with a screenshot and the person’s support ID (shown in your chat’s details, and in reports owners receive). We aim to respond within 24 hours. We can close an account that breaks these rules: it’s removed from every neighborhood, can’t send or receive messages, and its phone number can’t be used again.

Terms of use

Subscriptions. Joining a neighborhood is free. Owning one takes a yearly subscription, billed by Apple, that renews automatically until you cancel in your App Store settings. If a plan ends, the neighborhood keeps working for 30 days. After the owner’s first 7 days, any member can subscribe and become the owner. After 30 days it’s paused: nothing is deleted, but new plans, shared messages and new members wait until someone subscribes.

Using the app means agreeing to these guidelines and to Apple’s standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement. You must be at least 18 years old. We may close accounts that break these guidelines.
