How do I join my neighborhood?

Ask a neighbor who’s already in for the invite code or link. In the app, choose Join and enter the code. To start a new one, create a neighborhood in the app and share the code it gives you.

How do I start a private chat?

Pick a neighbor from your neighborhood’s member list, scan their QR code in person, or open an invite link they sent you.

Why do notifications just say “You have something new”?

Notifications pass through Apple’s servers, so we keep messages out of them. Open the app to see what arrived.

I got a new phone

Keep your old phone until you’ve moved. On the new phone, install the app and tap Moving from another phone?. It shows a code. On the old phone, go to Me → Move to a new phone and scan it. Your neighborhoods, chats and photos move over, encrypted with a key that’s only in that code, and the old phone is emptied.

If your old phone is lost or broken: if you turned on Me → Recovery backup, install the app on the new phone, tap Restore from a recovery code and enter the code you wrote down. Your neighborhoods, chats and plans come back (photos, voice messages and files don’t). Without a backup, start fresh and rejoin your neighborhood with its code.

Can I use it on my iPad or a second phone?

Yes, on up to 2 more devices. On the new device, tap Link to my account on another phone. On your phone, go to Me → Linked devices and scan its code. Linked devices get new messages from then on, not earlier chats.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Joining a neighborhood is free. Owning one takes a yearly plan billed by Apple. Manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings. Refunds are handled by Apple.

How do I block or report someone?

To block, open the chat’s details and choose Block. To report, long-press a message or choose “Report this plan.” Reports go to your neighborhood’s owners. See the community guidelines.

How do I report someone to you?

Email us a screenshot and their support ID. You’ll find it in the details of your chat with them. Your own support ID is under Me → Your account.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Me → Delete my account. Everything the server holds about you is erased right away. Read the privacy policy for the details.
